# CINEMATIC VIDEO BRIEF (hero + story)

Goal: 10-second loop plus 3-4 supporting clips that feel like a luxury campaign
(Apple launch film x Armani x Gucci x Rolex), not a corporate video.

## Style
Low-key lighting: one warm key light (tungsten ~3200K), deep shadows, black backgrounds or dark interiors.
Slow camera: gimbal push-ins, slider moves, macro details. 60fps shot, played at 40-50% speed.
No talking, no stock footage, no text in the first clips. Cinematic 2.39:1 crop OR 16:9 with letterboxing.
Color grade: crushed blacks, warm gold highlights, slight teal-free shadows, fine film grain.
Sound (optional, for a separate version): low ambient pad, a single soft tick, no music drop.

## Shot list (half a day, 1 videographer, 1 assistant)
1. HERO LOOP: her in dark clothing, side-lit, turning slowly toward the light; slow push-in on her face/eyes. 8-10s, seamless loop.
2. Hands: writing in a notebook / signing / turning a watch on the wrist. Macro.
3. City at night from a window: bokeh lights, slow rack focus.
4. Lighthouse / coastline at dusk (or a lamp beam in a dark room if no coast nearby) = the BEAM metaphor.
5. Walking shot from behind through a marble/dark corridor, light falling in stripes.
6. Fitness / focus moment: chalk, breath, a rope, a quiet gym in low light (for "Beyond the boardroom").
7. Laptop screen glow on her face, reflection in glasses (very subtle).
8. Black frame with the beam sweeping: can be built in code, no shoot needed.

## Wardrobe / styling
Black, charcoal, ivory, deep navy. One gold detail (watch, ring, or earring). Minimal makeup, natural hair, no logos.

## Technical delivery
- Shoot 4K 60fps (log profile if possible). Export web versions: 1920x1080, H.264 mp4 + VP9/AV1 webm, under 4 MB for hero (no audio track), plus a poster JPG.
- Mobile hero: 720x1280 vertical crop version, under 2.5 MB.
- Provide still photos too (portrait, hands, details) for OG image and gallery.

## Budget-friendly fallback
If no shoot is possible yet: build the hero from the animated beam on near-black plus one great portrait with slow zoom. Do NOT use generic stock business footage.

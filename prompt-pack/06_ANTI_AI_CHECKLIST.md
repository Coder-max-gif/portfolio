# "DOES THIS LOOK AI-MADE?" CHECKLIST
Review the finished site against every line. Fix anything that fails.

## Visual tells to AVOID
[ ] Purple/blue gradient blobs or glowing orbs
[ ] Three identical cards in a row
[ ] Emoji or generic icon packs (Lucide/Heroicons everywhere)
[ ] Everything centered, same spacing, same radius
[ ] Inter/Poppins on every element
[ ] Glass effect on everything
[ ] Stock photos of handshakes, skylines, laptops
[ ] Fake stats ("500+ clients") or fake testimonials
[ ] Buzzword copy: "unlock", "elevate", "empower", "seamless", "cutting-edge", "journey"
[ ] "Trusted by" logo strip with logos she has not approved

## Signs it feels human and expensive
[ ] Real photography and video of HER
[ ] Her actual words used (see 04_COPY_DECK.md), edited lightly
[ ] At least 3 places where the layout breaks the grid on purpose
[ ] Typography has hierarchy and contrast: one huge serif moment per section
[ ] Large empty areas: at least 30% of each screen is breathing room
[ ] Only ONE accent colour (gold) used with restraint
[ ] Animation timing is slow and consistent; no random bouncing
[ ] Custom details: cursor, hairlines, section numbering, grain
[ ] Hover states feel designed, not default
[ ] The beam concept appears at least in hero, one mid section and contact

## Quality gates
[ ] Looks excellent at 375px (phone), 768px (tablet), 1440px (laptop), 1920px+
[ ] Lighthouse: Performance 90+, Accessibility 95+, SEO 95+
[ ] No layout shift; fonts and video preloaded properly
[ ] prefers-reduced-motion respected
[ ] All [PLACEHOLDER] values replaced with verified facts
[ ] Legal/disclaimer text approved by her
[ ] Open Graph image checked when pasting the link in WhatsApp, LinkedIn, Instagram bio
[ ] Contact form tested end to end (email delivery + spam protection)

## Final test
Show the site to 3 people for 5 seconds. Ask: "What does she do? Would you trust her? Did a person design this?"
If any answer is "no" or "unsure", iterate.
